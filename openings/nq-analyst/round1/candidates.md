# Round 1 slate — hand-run (8 archetypes)

Because the CLI backend timed out on the wide funnel, this round was run
by hand. Eight deliberately distinct archetypes were drafted along the
role's likely hiring axes: contract-sizing posture, edge philosophy,
risk-first vs. edge-first framing, and how each treats prop-firm rules.

| id  | archetype                       | one-liner |
|-----|---------------------------------|-----------|
| c01 | Contract-sizing pragmatist      | Refuses to trade NQ mini at this DD; sizes on MNQ and treats the CEO's brief as the first thing to push back on. |
| c02 | Mean-reversion specialist       | Statistical, RTH-only, opening-range fade with hard stop; leans on regime filters. |
| c03 | Opening-drive momentum trader   | 09:30–10:30 ET only, first-hour trend continuation; wants tick data and realistic fills. |
| c04 | Adversarial skeptic             | Leads with "the math doesn't work as stated"; refuses to design a strategy until the DD or product is renegotiated. |
| c05 | Systems / risk-engine owner     | Talks entirely about pre-trade risk and kill-switch architecture; the edge is a plug-in. |
| c06 | Options-collar hybrid           | Trades MNQ with a defined-risk MES/MNQ options overlay to bound tail moves through events. |
| c07 | Overnight gap / ETH specialist  | Ignores the RTH consensus; targets Asia/London session mean-reversion in ETH where ranges are tighter. |
| c08 | ML classifier quant             | Feature-engineered daily-direction classifier with walk-forward; deeply cost-aware, sceptical of own model. |
