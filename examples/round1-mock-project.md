# Mock project: the momentum strategy that looks too good

A researcher has handed you a mean-reversion strategy on US equities. Their
backtest covers 2015-2024 and reports a Sharpe of 2.8, a max drawdown of 6%, and
a 71% hit rate. The universe is "liquid names"; positions are held 1-3 days and
sized equally. They used daily close data from a vendor feed, and they mention
in passing that they "cleaned out a few bad tickers" and "tried a bunch of
lookback windows before settling on 12 days."

They want to go live with $2M next month.

Talk me through how you would work this. I am not asking you to run anything — I
want to understand how you think, what you would look at first, what would make
you say no, and what it would take to change your mind.
